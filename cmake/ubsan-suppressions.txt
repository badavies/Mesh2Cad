# constructors of TopoDS_Shape and friends in Open CASCADE's own headers. Only alignment reports
# located in those headers are dropped. Remove this once the vcpkg baseline ships a fixed OCCT.

# is located in <bits/stl_algobase.h>, so it is matched by the function instead.
